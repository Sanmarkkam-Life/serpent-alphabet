## Group teachings (for the "Soul Letters" and "Body Letters" intro screens)
- **Soul letters:** in the word Tamil, "*A* and *i* are *citkalai*, powers of consciousness." (Vyakhyanangal 3.2.1; said of அ and இ in that word, not of all vowels)
- **Body letters:** in the word Tamil, த், ம், ழ் are "like the body to the life (*uyir*)". (Vyakhyanangal 3.4.1; said of those three, not of all consonants)
- **Tamil itself:** "the Tamil language itself gives the experience of pure Sivam most easily." (Vyakhyanangal 3.10.1)
- **A caution from Vallalar:** the letters are only "the first doorway"; in the end "one leaves off dwelling on the letters … so that compassion and Sivam alone are the reality." (Notes of Teaching 1.52.1)
